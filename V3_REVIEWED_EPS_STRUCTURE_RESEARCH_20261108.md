# Diagnose reviewed missing EPS without changing source observations

The November 6 operational ledger has 82 issuers with only the PER/EPS
subfactor incomplete. In 75 of them, original-file review found no additional
supported basic EPS. Repeating the same extractor cannot explain the gap.

A separate read-only probe selects at most four reviewed issuers, prioritizing
the single-EPS-gap group. It requires an independently retained exact-scope,
same-receipt annual CFO and official issuer identity, and checks original
archive/XML bounds. Only fixed-category anonymous counts are emitted:
standard basic versus diluted versus other/extension per-share concept hints,
exact annual periods, scope/dimension categories and per-share units. Literal
basic-EPS label resources are counted only as unbound research hints.

No issuer IDs, receipts, context IDs, arbitrary tag names, numerical facts or
raw originals are retained. The workflow has contents-read permission only,
no score dispatch, no source writer, no schedule and no automatic continuation.
Its anonymous artifact expires after 14 days. New basic-EPS concepts or share
class contexts cannot be promoted from these counts. Findings must first lead
to a separately tested source-proof contract; current EPS, reviews and scores
remain unchanged. The four samples are not a statistical recovery forecast.
